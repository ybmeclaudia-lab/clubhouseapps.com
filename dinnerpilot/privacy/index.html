Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

Clubhouse Apps ("we," "our," or "us") operates the DinnerPilot iOS application and the ClubhouseApps.com website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using DinnerPilot or this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and, if you sign in with Apple or Google, your name (if you choose to share it).

Family profile: To personalize your meal plans, we collect information you provide including family size, ages of children, dietary restrictions, disliked foods, cooking preferences, cuisine preferences, maximum cook time, and weekly grocery budget.

Meal and usage data: We store your saved meal plans, grocery lists, meal history, liked and disliked recipes, and in-app activity related to meal planning features.

Pantry Chef images: If you use the Pantry Chef camera feature (Pro), photos you take or select from your library are sent to our AI processing service to identify ingredients. These images are processed in real time and are not stored permanently by us or our AI provider.

Device and usage information: We collect limited technical information such as app version, device type, and crash reports to maintain service quality. We do not collect precise location data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate personalized weekly meal plans tailored to your family
  • To create and sync your grocery lists across devices
  • To power AI features including recipe generation and ingredient identification
  • To remember your preferences and improve recommendations over time
  • To manage your subscription and process payments via Apple's App Store
  • To send service-related communications (not marketing without your consent)
  • To display advertisements to free-tier users via Google AdMob
  • To diagnose crashes and maintain app stability

3. Third-Party Services

We share data with the following trusted third parties to operate our service:

  • Supabase — Cloud database and authentication provider. Stores your account and profile data securely. Data may be hosted in the United States or European Union.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — Processes AI requests including recipe generation and ingredient identification from photos. Ingredient text and images are sent to Anthropic's servers for processing. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to this processing.
  • Apple Sign-In — Optional sign-in method. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In — Optional sign-in method. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob — Advertising network used for free-tier users. AdMob may use device identifiers to serve ads. You will be asked for permission before personalized ads are shown (App Tracking Transparency). Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — Handles all subscription payments. We do not store your payment card information.
  • Cloudflare — CDN and security provider for this website.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Meal history is automatically pruned to 90 days. Week history is capped at 12 weeks. You may delete your account at any time, which permanently removes all associated data from our systems (see Section 8).

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

DinnerPilot is designed for use by adults and families, not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

6. Your Rights — GDPR (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format.
  • Objection: Object to certain processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at dave@clubhouseapps.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Your Rights — CCPA (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to request deletion, and the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell your personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us at dave@clubhouseapps.com.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your DinnerPilot account and all associated data at any time from within the app: go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Deletion permanently removes your profile, meal history, saved meals, and all other data from our servers. This action cannot be undone.

You may also request deletion by emailing dave@clubhouseapps.com. We will process your request within 30 days. For more information, see our Data Deletion Policy.

9. Cookies and Tracking (Website)

The ClubhouseApps.com website uses no advertising cookies or third-party tracking scripts. Cloudflare, our CDN provider, may set cookies for security and performance purposes. Our email capture forms use Formspree, which processes submitted email addresses according to Formspree's privacy policy.

10. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (TLS), row-level security on our database, and JWT-based authentication. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable precautions to protect your data.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will notify you within the app. Your continued use of DinnerPilot after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
