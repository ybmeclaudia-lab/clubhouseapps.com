Data Deletion Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

At Clubhouse Apps, we believe you should have complete control over your data. This page explains how to delete your DinnerPilot account and all associated personal data from our systems.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once deleted, your meal plans, grocery lists, preferences, meal history, and all other data cannot be recovered.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

  • Your account credentials and profile information
  • Family profile (family size, dietary restrictions, preferences)
  • Saved meal plans and grocery lists
  • Meal history and liked/disliked recipes
  • Weekly budget settings
  • All other personal data associated with your account

Data deletion is processed within 30 days of your request. Backups containing your data are purged within 90 days.

How to Delete Your Account

You have two ways to delete your account:

Option 1 — Delete from within the app (recommended)

1

Open DinnerPilot on your iPhone

2

Tap the Profile tab (bottom right)

3

Scroll down and tap Settings

4

Tap Delete Account

5

Confirm the deletion when prompted

Your account and all data will be permanently deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Submit a deletion request by email

If you cannot access the app, email us at dave@clubhouseapps.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm when deletion is complete.

Canceling Your Subscription

Deleting your DinnerPilot account does not automatically cancel your App Store subscription. To avoid future charges, cancel your subscription separately before deleting your account:

1

Open the Settings app on your iPhone

2

Tap your name at the top → Subscriptions

3

Find DinnerPilot Pro and tap Cancel Subscription

Data We Cannot Delete

In limited cases, we may retain certain data after account deletion:

  • Records required for legal compliance (e.g., billing records for tax purposes)
  • Anonymized, aggregated data that cannot be linked back to you
  • Data required to resolve ongoing disputes or enforce our Terms of Service

Questions

If you have questions about data deletion or your privacy rights, contact us at dave@clubhouseapps.com. For more information, see our Privacy Policy.
