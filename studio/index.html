Common questions

Real questions from real business owners.

Do I own the website?

Yes — the code, the content, and your domain are all yours from day one. With The Handoff, it's a one-time project: you pay, we launch, you walk away with everything. With the Care Plan, you still own the site outright; you just commit to 12 months of maintenance so the relationship has time to deliver the ongoing service the plan promises. After your first year, you can take the site anywhere you want with 30 days notice. No hostage situations either way.

What if I already have a domain?

Great. We use it. I'll walk you through pointing it at your new site (usually a 5-minute DNS change). You keep ownership of the domain in your name.

Do I need to provide my own photos?

Yes. You provide the photos of your team, your work, your shop, your space — whatever's right for the site. I'll size, crop, and place them. If you don't have good photos yet, I'll point you at some clean stock options to use as placeholders until you do.

How long does it take?

Most 5-page sites go live within two to three weeks of the kickoff call. Faster if you have your content and photos ready. Slower if we need to do a photo day or write copy from scratch.

What happens if I want to cancel the Care Plan?

The Care Plan has a 12-month minimum because the $500 build fee assumes I'll be supporting the site for the year. After your first year, cancel anytime with 30 days notice — you take the files, point your domain wherever you want, and we're square. No long contracts beyond that first year.

Do you do e-commerce or booking systems?

Not as a primary build. Simple Stripe payment links or Calendly embeds? Yes. Full e-commerce stores or custom booking software? That's outside the scope of what I do well — I'll refer you to someone who specializes.
